Welcome to the College of Engineering Perumon

College of Engineering Perumon is a government-controlled engineering college established in 2000 under the Co-operative Academy of Professional Education (CAPE). It is affiliated to APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University and approved by AICTE. The college is known for its commitment to quality education, technical innovation, and student success.

Departments

  • Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
  • Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)
  • Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE)
  • Mechanical Engineering (ME)

Principal's Message

Welcome to the College of Engineering Perumon. Our institution is committed to fostering an environment of academic excellence, innovation, and integrity. We aim to equip our students with the skills and knowledge necessary to meet global challenges and contribute meaningfully to society.
